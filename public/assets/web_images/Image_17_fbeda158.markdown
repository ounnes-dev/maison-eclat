## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the convergence of French clinical dermatology and understated haute-lifestyle publishing. Drawing inspiration from independent art-of-living journals (*Cereal*, *Kinfolk*) and Parisian haute-parfumerie aesthetics, the visual language prioritizes architectural balance, intellectual restraint, and sensual precision over commercial spa tropes.

### Core Attributes
- **Artisanal Clinical**: Uncompromising European skincare expertise delivered with warm, tactile humanity rather than antiseptic coldness.
- **Poetic Architecture**: Deliberate negative space, asymmetric editorial layout grids, hairline framing, and rhythmic interplay of high-contrast display typography.
- **Sensory Subtlety**: Powdered pigments, natural linens, and unhurried visual cadence designed to lower cortisol and convey quiet authority.

### Stylistic Philosophy
A hybrid of **Warm Minimalist Editorial** and **Refined Tactility**. Elements reject ubiquitous round pill buttons, heavy dropped shadows, artificial gold foil gradients, or generic wellness tropes. Hierarchy is sculpted through deliberate typographic scale, hairline structural divisions, and organic tone-on-tone layering.

## Colors

The palette derives from raw mineral pigments, untreated parchment, and deep espresso inks. Contrast is sharp yet organic, trading synthetic blacks and stark whites for historic atelier finishes.

### Roles and Application
- **Primary (`#2B211C` — Espresso Brun)**: The anchoring tone. Used for dominant typographic copy, architectural keylines, high-priority actions, and structured iconographies.
- **Secondary (`#B76E79` — Muted Rose)**: The emotional accent. Applied sparingly for active tabs, selected states, sensitive clinical callouts, and subtle hover revelations. Never used as heavy solid backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#8A7A6B` — Taupe Minéral)**: The textural intermediary. Used for secondary metadata, captions, inactive borders, and gentle dividers.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F1E8` — Ivoire Chaud)**: The foundational backdrop. Emulates heavyweight uncoated art paper, casting a warm ambient base across all primary screens.
- **Neutral Surface (`#EDE6DA` — Beige Doux)**: The contextual substrate for cards, inset panels, consultation summaries, and elevated drawers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy constructs an editorial conversation between classic French literary serif typography (`Playfair Display`) and systematic Swiss functionalism (`Inter`).

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Large Headlines**: Set in regular weights with subtle tracking pull. To evoke high-end print publishing, feature italic phrases within primary display headings (e.g., *le rituel signature*) to introduce poetic emphasis.
- **Editorial Sub-headers**: Set with breathing room, functioning as narrative guideposts rather than technical markers.
- **Body Text**: Rendered in light to regular weights using `Inter` with generous leading (`1.6` to `1.7x`) to ensure prolonged readability during clinical consultations and diagnostic reviews.
- **Labels, Trackers & Monograms**: Cast in small uppercase sizes with generous kerning (`letter-spacing: 0.08em - 0.12em`) to mirror luxury catalog pagination and treatment codes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the rhythm of a high-end publication: deliberate white space, asymmetric two- and three-column balances, and structural framing.

### Layout Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: A 12-column dynamic grid bounded by `margin-lg` (4rem/64px) and structured by `gutter-lg` (2.5rem/40px). Text narratives frequently offset across columns 2-7, while imagery spans columns 8-12, generating asymmetric editorial balance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: An 8-column layout utilizing `margin-md` (2.5rem/40px) and `gutter` (1.5rem/24px) for measured content consolidation.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: A 4-column column layout anchored by `margin` (1.25rem/20px). Spacing collapses from sprawling architectural fields to disciplined, vertical cadences.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical pacing prioritizes generous intervals between distinct protocol chapters (`space-xl`), allowing users to pause and absorb clinical details.
- Internal component rhythm follows tight increments (`space-xs` through `space-md`) to ensure inputs and service selectors feel unified and intentional.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids drop shadows and floating elevations. Spatial separation is achieved through tactile, print-inspired mechanisms:

### Tonal Tiers
Depth relies on surface shifts rather than optical detachment:
1. **Base Ground**: `Ivoire Chaud` (`#F7F1E8`) establishes the floor.
2. **Layered Plinth**: `Beige Doux` (`#EDE6DA`) provides an offset tone for treatment cards, diagnostic dossiers, and formula summaries.
3. **Elevated Panels**: Flyout menus, booking drawers, and modal sheets use the same base tone but are bordered by subtle tonal keylines, resting flush against content.

### Hairline Borders
Visual structure is defined through razor-sharp lines (`1px solid rgba(43, 33, 28, 0.12)` or `rgba(138, 122, 107, 0.22)`). Cards and sections are separated like book leaf sections rather than suspended UI cards.

### Minimal Diffused Shadow
When accessibility requires temporary modal isolation, use a singular low-amplitude, warm-tinted shadow:
`0 16px 32px -8px rgba(43, 33, 28, 0.08)`. Never use cold gray or high-opacity black shadows.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft Architectural** geometry (`roundedness: 1`), utilizing minimal radius values (2px to 4px) that feel pressed, clipped, and tailor-made.

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Sharp Corners (0px)**: Used for full-width section dividing blocks, editorial hero imagery, table grids, and canvas bounds to echo magazine spreads.
- **Soft Edge (4px / 0.25rem)**: Standard treatment for interactive cards, input frames, dropdown menus, and standard buttons. Imparts warmth without appearing playful or synthetic.
- **Restraint on Rounding**: Full pills and circular containers are strictly reserved for functional round icons (e.g., play state, step indicators). Rounded capsules are disallowed for action buttons to preserve clinical rigor.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#2B211C` background with `#F7F1E8` text. Rectangular with minimal radius (`roundedness: 1`), generous horizontal padding (`space-lg`), and crisp uppercase typographic treatment (`label-md`). Hover shifts subtly to an espresso tint with an imperceptible inward scale.
- **Secondary Button**: Outlined with a 1px hairline border (`#8A7A6B`), transparent interior, and `#2B211C` text. Hover fills the background with `rgba(183, 110, 121, 0.08)` (Muted Rose tint).
- **Text Link / Ghost Action**: Inline typography paired with an underline offset by 4px. On hover, the underline transitions into `#B76E79`.

### Chips & Protocol Tags
- Compact tags indicate treatment durations, skin typologies, and atelier locations (Paris / Bruxelles).
- Bordered with ultra-fine `#8A7A6B` outlines on `#EDE6DA` backgrounds. Typography uses `label-sm` in all caps. Never styled as bulbous pills.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Flat surfaces embedded into `#EDE6DA` or bordered strictly at the base using a single bottom hairline border.
- Floating labels in `Inter` (`body-sm`), transitioning to `label-sm` in `#8A7A6B` on focus.
- Focused state highlights the border with `#2B211C` without outer glow rings. Error states use a subdued crimson rust rather than loud neon red.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom thin-ruled square selectors (16px) with an interior inset mark in `#2B211C`.
- Radio selections feature a crisp concentric ring without gradient fills.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Flat, non-elevated structures defined by `1px solid rgba(138, 122, 107, 0.2)` on `#EDE6DA` foundations.
- Header zones contain an asymmetric combination: an editorial serif title accompanied by an uppercase monospaced treatment code (`label-sm`).
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg`) prevents elements from crowding borders.

### Diagnostic Sliders & Accordions
- Accordion dividers feature extended horizontal hairlines. Expand triggers utilize delicate `+` and `—` glyphs instead of chevron arrows.
- Diagnostic scale sliders use a single hairline track in `#8A7A6B` with a solid rectangular `#2B211C` scrub thumb.
